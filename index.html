Where's the leverage?

Every flow's leak, summed across all 13 mapped journeys — five ways to look at the same data, to open the conversation about what's worth attacking first. Weight = how many of the 13 flows carry that data to that place, not raw request count. One honest caveat: 9 of the 13 journeys ride MetaMask, so "reach" partly reflects what we've mapped so far — treat the counts as an initial signal, weight later by real transaction volume.
